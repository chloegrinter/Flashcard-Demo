[Documentation Process for the Flashcard Game]
I want to have an easily accessible way to store revision notes.
Normally, people make flashcards, however I think there are some flaws to using them:
	- You can Misplace them and all of your progress is gone
	- They take up alot of space
	- Its kinda easy to just flip over the card when you can't be bothered answering the question
	
Then, I realised i could use the philosophy of flashcards, but on a device that I'm always carrying around.
Therefore, I won't misplace my revision technique, It doesn't take up space and I can program it to cover for laziness.

[Project Outline]
I want to be able to / Key Features:
	- Write Questions and Answers, which are then stored to an SQLite Database
	(I think SQLite is optimal here because I won't need to connect to a database server)
	- Answer randomised questions based on what I've already written

Desirable Features:
	- Tracking How many Questions I got right / Wrong
	- Tracking which Questions I get wrong most often for further revision
	- Maybe linking craig n dave revision videos / classroom powerpoints for topics I struggle with.
